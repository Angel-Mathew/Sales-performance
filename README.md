# Sales Performance Dashboard

A sales dashboard that tracks revenue by product category, region and sales representative over several years, with a monthly view.

## The problem
A sales team needs to know which products, regions and people drive revenue, and when sales go up or down during the year. I built this to make those answers visible without digging through data.

## What you can explore
- Sales and profit by product category (Stationery, Technology, Office Supplies, Furniture)
- Sales by month, and by month and product
- Sales by sales representative and by region
- Filters for region, product and month or year

## What I found
- Regions are close. West leads at about 0.15M, then North and East at about 0.14M each, and South at about 0.13M.
- Sales reps are balanced. The individual sales people each bring in about 31K to 33K.
- Monthly sales stay in a narrow range, with no single month dominating.
